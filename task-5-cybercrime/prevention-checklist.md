Title: Stay Safe Online – Cybercrime Prevention Checklist

✔ Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, or passwords with anyone
✔ Do not click on unknown links or suspicious messages
✔ Always verify the identity of callers claiming to be from banks or companies
✔ Use strong and unique passwords for all accounts
✔ Enable two-factor authentication wherever possible
✔ Avoid using public Wi-Fi for financial transactions

✔ (UPI Safety) Never approve a “collect request” unless you are sure
✔ (UPI Safety) Remember: receiving money does NOT require entering your PIN

✔ Keep your apps and phone updated regularly
✔ Log out from shared or public devices

📢 Report Cybercrime:

* National Cyber Crime Portal: https://cybercrime.gov.in
* Helpline Number: 1930
