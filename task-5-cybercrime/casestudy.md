Title: UPI Payment Fraud Case Study

UPI payment fraud is one of the most common cybercrimes affecting students and the general public in India. It usually involves scammers tricking victims into sharing sensitive information or authorizing fraudulent transactions.

In a typical case, the scammer contacts the victim through a phone call, SMS, or social media, pretending to be a bank official, delivery agent, or buyer. They may send a fake payment request link or QR code and ask the victim to “accept” it to receive money. In reality, accepting the request results in money being debited from the victim’s account. In some cases, scammers also ask for OTPs or UPI PINs under false pretenses.

Students, job seekers, and people unfamiliar with digital payment systems are the most common targets. The consequences can include financial loss, emotional stress, and in severe cases, identity theft.

This type of fraud highlights the importance of awareness and caution while using digital payment platforms. Users must understand that receiving money never requires entering a PIN or approving a request, and any such demand is likely a scam.
